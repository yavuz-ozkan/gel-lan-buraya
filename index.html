How to play

Noah

  • Get out the front door!
  • Push the stick a little to tiptoe.
  • Push it all the way to run. Running is loud!
  • Creaky floor and squeaky toys are loud too.
  • When Baba's eye opens, stand still!
  • Sneak quietly to win stars. A silent sneak gets 3!
  • At the door, pick a prank. It keeps Baba stuck for a bit. Each prank works once per house.
  • You can crawl under the tables. Baba can't!

Baba

  • You are asleep. Mostly.
  • Tap Peek to open one eye. You get 3 peeks a night.
  • If Noah moves while your eye is open, back to bed he goes!
  • After the prank you are stuck for a moment. Then chase him!
  • Catch Noah before he gets out the front door!

Keys: arrow keys to move. Enter or Space to peek.

Baba is the computer
